Calling the Tillers simply a "faction" sounds misleading--gaining reputation with [The Tillers](/faction=1272/the-tillers), in Valley of the Four Winds, is much more complex than any other Pandaria faction. You'll harvest a variety of crops yielding everything from vegetables to profession materials, gain reputation through a series of quests to expand your farm, and also gain friendship with 10 Tiller NPCs who enhance your farm in Halfhill.  
  
There's a ton of stuff to cover in this preview, so we've broken it down into three tabbed sections:  


  * **Farming** : how to plant, grow, and maintain crops, as well as how to expand a farm.
  * **Tillers Union** : covers the main questline in which the player wins union votes from Halfhill residents, daily quests, and Tillers reputation rewards.
  * **Friendship** : the friendship reputation system, how to increase friendship through quests and gifts, as well as Best Friend rewards.

  
  


## Patch 9.0.1 Changes

  
With the Shadowlands pre-patch, the entirety of Pandaria now scales from level 10 to 50 along side the character. This has resulted in a number of changes with how this section of the game operates:  


  * Reputation quests are now available from level 15.
  * Flying is not available to characters until level 30, meaning some of the reputation quests are not soloable until level 30.

## Farming Your Crop

  


  * Before you start, [Farmer Yoon](/npc=58730/farmer-yoon) will give you a [series of quests](http://mop.wowhead.com/search?q=learn+and+grow) preparing you to manage your farm.
  * Your crop is a phased area unique to you by Sunsong Ranch (unexpected bonus in PvP areas). You start off with four soil patches, but it looks like it can be increased up to 16 as reputation is unlocked.
  * Buy seeds from [Merchant Greenfield](/npc=58718/merchant-greenfield) in Halfhill Market, and plant them in your patch of soil (click on the patch) at Sunsong Ranch. Higher reputation levels unlock more seed types.
  * When the crop is "growing," it is in danger of requiring maintenance (see next section). Tend to your crop and it should be good to go.
  * After a day or so (there's inconsistent growth patterns), you can harvest the crop.
  * Harvesting the crop will yield 50 reputation at level 90 (no reputation below), and vegetables as well as a chance of additional seeds.
  * [Jogu the Drunk](/npc=58710/jogu-the-drunk) will make a prediction as to which crops will yield good harvests tomorrow. However, if you are not Best Friends with Jogu he will charge you an amount of money to make the prediction, starting at and reducing down for each level of friendship.
  * If you want to plant something else, [Dented Shovel](/item=89880/dented-shovel) removes the existing crop, but your initial seeds will be lost.
  * You can start tilling at level 10, but you won't gain reputation for crops until level 15.

  
  


## Planting Your Crop

  
There are three types of seeds to plant, all sold by [Merchant Greenfield](/npc=58718/merchant-greenfield):  


  * **Vegetable Seeds** used in Cooking cost --this should help out with Cooking ingredients too. They can grow all vegetables.
  * **Blossom Tree Seeds** cost and are available at various reputation levels. Planting these seeds allows you, one day later, to harvest an item that plants a tree, lasting for 3 minutes anywhere in the world.
  * **Profession Seeds** require **Revered** with [The Tillers](/faction=1272/the-tillers) and cost . These seeds passively acquire various profession reagents.

  * Crops can be in one of 10 negative states that need your assistance to be remedied. 8 are found in freshly-planted crops, while an additional 2 can be encountered between harvesting and tilling.
  * [Farmer Yoon](/npc=58730/farmer-yoon) offers some farming tips, but they're more confusing riddles instead of helpful advice.

## Expanding Your Farm

  


  * All players start out with 4 soil squares on their farm. You'll most likely reach 16 soil squares at least.
  * Completing the quest chain for the Tillers Union, in which you befriend and gain votes in Halfhill Market, will unlock more squares. 
  * At **Honored** , you can clear weeds for [Growing the Farm](/spell=114431/growing-the-farm):  
[Growing the Farm I: The Weeds](/quest=30260/growing-the-farm-i-the-weeds)
  * [Growing the Farm I: A Little Problem](/quest=30516/growing-the-farm-i-a-little-problem)

  
At **Revered** , you can move a cart out of the way.  


  * [Growing the Farm II: The Broken Wagon](/quest=30523/growing-the-farm-ii-the-broken-wagon)
  * [Growing the Farm II: Knock on Wood](/quest=30524/growing-the-farm-ii-knock-on-wood)

  
At **Exalted** , you can move a huge boulder out of the way for the last 4 squares: [Growing the Farm III: The Mossy Boulder](/quest=30529/growing-the-farm-iii-the-mossy-boulder)  
There are three items that simplify running your farm. They can be purchased from [Gina Mudclaw](/npc=66832/gina-mudclaw) and used after completing quest objectives related to fixing your farm.  


  * ["Jinyu Princess" Irrigation System](/item=89812/jinyu-princess-irrigation-system): Requires weeds cleared from farm. This creates a sprinkler system that waters up to 8 parched crops at once.
  * ["Thunder King" Pest Repellers](/item=89813/thunder-king-pest-repellers): Requires broken wagon fixed. This creates a pesticide system that sprays up to 8 infected crops at once.
  * ["Earth-Slasher" Master Plow](/item=89814/earth-slasher-master-plow): Requires mossy boulder removed. This allows you to plow four crops at once.

  
Gaining friendship with TIller NPCs (see below) will improve your farm in various small ways. For example, one NPC will add chickens to your farm.

## Friendship with Tiller NPCs

  
  


## Overview and Rewards

  


  * 10 Tiller NPCs have Friendship bars, which are similar to reputation but don't have normal Normal/Friendly distinctions. Instead, there are six segments of 8400 friendship each, with the best level obtainable at 42000 friendship. While gaining friendship, you'll also gain some Tillers reputation.
  * You can check up on your friendship status at the Tillers Shrine in Sunsong Ranch.
  * Friendship levels:  
Stranger: 0-8400
  * Acquaintance: 8400-16800
  * Buddy: 16800-25200
  * Friend: 25200-33600
  * Good Friend: 33600-42000
  * Best Friend: 42000-42999

  
How to gain Friendship:  


  * The easiest way is to give each NPC 5 of their favorite food each day. Completing these meal quests will grant 1,800 friendship.
  * A harder way to gain reputation is to give friends specific gifts. These are found in piles of [Dark Soil](/object=210582/dark-soil) to the north of Halfhill which unfortunately cannot be tracked on the minimap. Each friend will grant 540 friendship per gift. In addition, there's one gift each NPC **really** wants, and turning that item into the specific NPC will grant 900 friendship instead.
  * Some quests give friendship--either quests that signal you've reached a new friendship level or select dailies.

  
As for rewards, you get some cute perks for becoming Best Friends. For example:  


  * Best Friends with [Old Hillpaw](/faction=1276/old-hillpaw) will add chickens to your farm and send you [Straw Hat](/item=90042/straw-hat).
  * [Ella](/faction=1275/ella) adds Luna the cat to your farm, and sends [Tree Seed Pack](/item=85272/tree-seed-pack) with Blossom seeds inside.
  * [Fish Fellreed](/faction=1282/fish-fellreed) adds 3 pink pigs and mails [Special Seed Pack](/item=85227/special-seed-pack), which has profession seeds inside.
  * [Farmer Fung](/faction=1283/farmer-fung) adds Shaggy the Prize Yak and sends [Enigma Seed](/item=85216/enigma-seed).
  * [Gina Mudclaw](/faction=1281/gina-mudclaw) adds a mailbox and mails [Celebration Gift](/item=85276/celebration-gift) which has fireworks inside.
  * [Sho](/faction=1278/sho) mails a [Red Cricket](/item=85222/red-cricket) and adds an orange tree to the farm.
  * [Chee Chee](/faction=1277/chee-chee) sends [Chee Chee's Goodie Bag](/item=85275/chee-chees-goodie-bag) which has cloth and leather, and adds sheep to your farm.
  * [Haohan Mudclaw](/faction=1279/haohan-mudclaw) sends 3 [Songbell Seed](/item=89233/songbell-seed) and adds "Miss Fifi," a mushan pet, to your farm.
  * [Jogu the Drunk](/faction=1273/jogu-the-drunk) sends [Secret Stash](/item=85271/secret-stash) with alcohol in the mail and won't charge you anything for crop predictions.

Some of the info in this guide is slightly off, and anno 8.0 I think some rep flow info for players that want to unlock the farm is in order. I assume you **do** have [Grand Commendation of the Tillers](/item=93226/grand-commendation-of-the-tillers), if you're doing it on your first character this whole process will take twice as long.  


  1. **Neutral** : the Tillers starts with [A Helping Hand](/quest=30252/a-helping-hand) at Farmer Yoon. Doing this whole chain will get you approximately to Honored.
  2. **Honored** : starts off with [Growing the Farm I: The Weeds](/quest=30260/growing-the-farm-i-the-weeds) for 2k rep and more slots. Requires 15 minutes login time. Then unlocks ["Jinyu Princess" Irrigation System](/item=89812/jinyu-princess-irrigation-system).
  3. **Honored ~50%** : at 5.6k you will get [Mung-Mung's Vote I: A Hozen's Problem](/quest=30258/mung-mungs-vote-i-a-hozens-problem) for 1.6k rep, requires **2x Juicycrunch Carrot** planted.
  4. **Revered** : starts off with [Growing the Farm II: The Broken Wagon](/quest=30523/growing-the-farm-ii-the-broken-wagon) for 2k rep and more slots. Requires 15 minutes login time. Then unlocks ["Thunder King" Pest Repellers](/item=89813/thunder-king-pest-repellers).
  5. **Revered 20%** : 4.2k will unlock [Farmer Fung's Vote I: Yak Attack](/quest=30517/farmer-fungs-vote-i-yak-attack) for 1.6k/rep, requires **3x Green Cabbage** planted.
  6. **Revered 40%** : 8.4k will unlock [Nana's Vote I: Nana's Secret Recipe](/quest=30519/nanas-vote-i-nanas-secret-recipe) for 1.6k/rep, requires **3x Witchberry** planted.
  7. **Revered 60%** : 12.6k will unlock [Lost and Lonely](/quest=30526/lost-and-lonely) for 1k/rep, theoretically this is **optional** , but don't you dare!
  8. **Revered 80%** : 16.8k will unlock [Haohan's Vote I: Bungalow Break-In](/quest=30521/haohans-vote-i-bungalow-break-in) for 4.4k/rep, requires **4x Striped Melons** planted, netting you **Exalted**.
  9. **Exalted** : will unlock [Growing the Farm III: The Mossy Boulder](/quest=30529/growing-the-farm-iii-the-mossy-boulder) to max out the farm after 15 minutes, also unlocking ["Earth-Slasher" Master Plow](/item=89814/earth-slasher-master-plow).

  
The Neutral chain doesn't exactly net Honored (don't have a character left that has to start from scratch), but considering it is (and it is close), you'll get **23.2/42k** from base to Exalted just from the chain. The remaining 19k can be obtained from dailies and harvests:  


  1. Any set of dailies nets **2.1k rep**.
  2. Any harvest nets **100 rep** , so 400 for a Friendly farm, 800 for a Honored farm and 1200 for a Revered farm.
  3. You can force rep by re-planting crops until you get a **Bursting** crop for 100 each, but this isn't a viable way to farm.

  
All in all, on an alt you'd require about **a week** to get to Exalted, which is not terrible for the benefit of a fully developed farm.

 _**How to get to Skyrange (or any area where flight seems to be required) without flight**_  
  
Now that the Skyrange quests are only level 15 you might be wondering how to get your lowbie up there:  
  


  1. Get your toon somewhat near your goal location and near a quick death (from mobs or a fall).
  2. Queue for both dungeons AND battlegrounds.
  3. Let your toon die.
  4. Fly your ghost toward Skyrange.
  5. If a queue pops before you get there, let it hang until it expires.
  6. Once you've reached your goal location, accept a queue pop.
  7. Either play out your dungeon/BG or just quit it (gives you debuff and maybe irks others though).
  8. Either way, you'll materialize alive and right where you wanted to be.

Ways to buffs your Tillers reputation gains include:  


  * For alts, buy [Grand Commendation of the Tillers](/item=93226/grand-commendation-of-the-tillers) from [Gina Mudclaw](/npc=58706/gina-mudclaw) in Halfhill Market on your main once you have Revered with the Tillers and right click on the item to use it. This grants a battle net account-wide buff to Tillers reputation gains, including from Revered to Exalted on your main. Note there is nothing to stop you from buying the item twice in error and it is NOT refundable. To check if you have it already, carry out any Tillers quest or harvest a crop from your farm. If the chat message is "Reputation with the Tillers increased by X. (+X/2 bonus) then you have already used it (there's no longer a golden arrow on the Reputation tab to show it after Shadowlands expac).
  * Monthly Darkmoon Faire [WHEE!](/spell=46668/whee) buff from using the Roller Coaster or Roundabout.
  * Once every 7 months (as of Shadowlands), [MoP Timewalking](https://www.wowhead.com/mists-of-pandaria-timewalking-guide) week. You can use the badges earned to buy Tiller's commendations from the Timewalking vendor on the Timeless Isle (Tushi's Landing flight path). Note that there is no flying on the Timeless Isle and if flying in manually, you will be dismounted when you get near. It also grants max level characters the buff [Sign of the Mists](/spell=335151/sign-of-the-mists) which increases the reputation earned from MoP factions.
  * Annual World Event buffs [The Spirit of Sharing](/spell=61849/the-spirit-of-sharing) and [Unburdened](/spell=95987/unburdened)

  
  
The [Celebration Package](/item=139285/celebration-package) and [Battle Standard of Coordination](/item=64399/battle-standard-of-coordination) only grant rep on kills, so aren't of use for Tillers.
