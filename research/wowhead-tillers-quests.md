Overview of The Tillers in MoP Classic
The Tillers activities will mostly be centered around farming on Sunsong Ranch, gathering special treats and items for your friends, and completing Daily quests to gain reputation in order to progress the Union questline. Cooking is not required to progress with the faction, but it does make life easier. Two cooking-specific recipes are also gated behind this reputation.

While you'll be able to do the introductory quests right at level 85, the valuable parts of the reputation are all gated behind quests only available at level 90, so I wouldn't recommend working towards this profession until you reach level 90.

Your reputation with The Tillers will be separate from that of your friends at the Halfhill Market.
The Tillers Location
The Tillers are situated in central Valley of the Four Winds at Halfhill. The primary quest and quartermaster location will be at the Halfhill Market, with your farm being located slightly north at Sunsong Ranch.

The Tillers Farming

Reins of the Azure Water Strider


After completing the initial questline, you'll have access to 4 plots to grow your crops. This expands to 16 plots as you progress through the union questline. In these plots, you can plant seeds sold by Merchant Greenfield, which will at first grant you basic cooking materials but then give more interesting materials, like Mote of Harmony Mote of Harmony, as you gain reputation with The Tillers.

It's not as simple as just planting the seed and gaining your reward, however. You'll need to till your soil and make sure all weeds and vermin are cleared. After planting your seed, you can run into some problems that need to be rectified if you wish to harvest your crop.

Infested crop - Use Vintage Bug Sprayer Vintage Bug Sprayer on the crop
Tangled crop - Right-click crop and move away
Runty crop - Right-click and jump
Allruing crop - Right-click and kill bird that spawns
Wiggling crop - Right-click and kill vermin that spawns
Parched crop - Use Rusty Watering Can Rusty Watering Can on the crop
Smothered crop - Right-click and keep pressing 1
Wild crop - Right-click and keep pressing 1, press 2 when  Vine Slam is cast

It'll generally take a day before your plants become harvestable. You can left-click your crop and hover over the buff to check the time until you can harvest. There's also a chance your crop grows into a bursting plant, making it harvestable immediately.

Taking Ownership of your Farm in MoP Classic
From Phase 3 of Mists of Pandaria Classic, you can take ownership of your farm from The Tillers. To complete this, you must have achieved Exalted status with The Tillers and have unlocked all 16 plots on your farm.

Once you have joined the Council and are an owner, Work Orders will unlock for various factions. These Work Orders are daily quests that reward.
